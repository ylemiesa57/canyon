How it works

  1. 0:00

    Upload

    STEP, IGES, native CAD, the bubbled print, your terms sheet. Canyon reads all of them.

  2. 1:30

    Read the band

    A price band, a lead time, and the manufacturability findings that move either one.

  3. Same day

    Release

    One click to the verified shops that qualify, then the agents run the rounds.

  4. Day one

    Award

    Offers ranked on your own weights. One shop, or split the order across two.

  1. 0:00

  3. 4:00
